# Two diagnostic tests on the final model: protocol

Written on 2026-11-01, after the final results and before computing either test. Decision of the user. The road network is not changed in any run. Neither test changes the final model: any change is a separate decision of the user. All detectors have been seen, so both tests are exploratory.

## Test 1: what explains the error at single roads (road_error_diagnosis.py, no simulations)
Outcome: y = log10(sim/obs) of daily volume, before detector correction, and |y|. Monday and Saturday groups of February 2020, all complete detectors; replication on the mean over the 2021 days (not independent: same detectors, same demand).
Predictor groups, fixed here: class (OSM road class); province; network (log length, edge betweenness of the model network with length as cost); parallel (model links within 1 km, not touching the link, bearing within 30 degrees: count and length); omitted (length within 1 km of OSM roads farther than 30 m from any model link: motorway to tertiary; unclassified and residential; the part of both within 30 degrees of the link); activity (ISTAT 2021 population and OSM shop, amenity and office points within 1 and 5 km, point features only); boundary (distance to the regional boundary).
Measures: out-of-fold R2 (OLS, 5 folds, 100 repeats) of each group alone; loss of out-of-fold R2 when the group is removed from the full model, with permutation p (999 permutations of the group's rows, Holm within outcome); leave-one-province-out R2; random forest out-of-bag R2 as a check for non-linearity.
Reading rule: a group is called relevant if, for y or |y| on Monday, its removal loses at least 0.05 of R2 with Holm p < 0.05, and at least 0.02 on 2021 for the same outcome. Province factors were fitted on these residuals, so the province group is expected to be small. A relevant omitted group would point to the network as a cause, and would be reported as a limit, with no roads added.

## Test 2: GPS weight per link in Forli-Cesena (link_weight_test.py, 2 simulations)
Change: in the weight file, links of Forli-Cesena with at least 50 GPS probes over the 8 days of February 2020 and unchanged geometry get a weight from their own daily count on the scale of the class weights, max(1, round(10 * count / 13.5)), in both directions; all other links keep the class weight. Same demand, k, factors, radius, shift, alpha = 0.5, alpha_s = 0.25.
Runs: Monday and Saturday. The second seed of the Monday baseline, already available, measures noise.
Primary evaluation: the detectors of Forli-Cesena (not used to build the weights): Spearman and dispersion of daily volumes against the final model, paired bootstrap over detectors (5000), February 2020 and 2021. Control: detectors outside the province. Manipulation check, not evidence (in sample): Spearman between simulated and GPS daily volume on the changed links.
Reading rule: the weights help if the interval of the Spearman difference is above zero or that of the dispersion difference below zero on both Monday and Saturday of February 2020, with the same sign in 2021. About 30 detectors: power is low, and an inconclusive result is not evidence of no effect.
